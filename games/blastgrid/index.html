How to Play

Drop bombs to blast soft crates and rival bombers. Fire travels in a cross and stops at pillars.

Move: Arrows / WASD. Hold to glide; buffer a side key for classic corner turns.

Space drops a bomb (up to your bomb cap). Pick up blue (+bomb), orange (+range), and teal (+speed) power-ups from destroyed crates.

Last bomber standing wins the round. First to 3 round wins takes the match. When the timer hits zero, sudden death closes the walls.
