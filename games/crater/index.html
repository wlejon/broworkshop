How to Play

2–6 player turn-based artillery. Destroy every other tank to win.

Aim: ↑/↓ pitch, ←/→ direction, Q/E power down/up.

Fire: Space. Projectile physics carves craters; tanks lose ground and fall.

All clients share a seeded heightmap — explosions look identical for everyone.

Host a match from the launcher (server auto-starts), then Connect.
