How to Play

Grab and drag any puff. Drag sideways to slide its whole row — puffs leaving one side come back on the other. Drag up or down to slide its column.

Let go to snap. Any line of 3+ same-color puffs pops; the rest fall and new puffs float in from the top.

Scoring: 50 per puff · cascades multiply x1, x2, x3…

Specials
4 in a line → Jumbo Puff (clears 3x3)
5 in a line → Arrow Puff (clears its row or column)
L or T shape → Prism Puff (clears its whole color)

Locked puffs wear a clamp and freeze their row and column. Pop them in a match to set them free.

Mouse     drag a puff; the direction locks
