How to Play

Gravity pulls the ball down through a field of pegs. Every peg it hits lights up and bursts at the end of the shot.

Blue — bonus score
Orange — clear all to win the level
Green — triggers your guide's ability
Purple — doubles score for the rest of the shot

A catch bar slides along the bottom: land in it for a free ball. Every 10 oranges cleared earns a bonus ball.
