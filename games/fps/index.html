How to Play

Connect to a local or remote FPS Arena server. Fight other players and AI bots in a small arena.

WASD / Arrows   Move
Mouse           Look
Click / Fire    Shoot
Esc / P         Pause (releases mouse)
Click canvas    Capture mouse

Start the server with:

bro-server games/fps server.js --tickrate 60

Default address: 127.0.0.1:27015
