How to Play

Chain touching letter tiles (sideways or diagonal) into English words of 3+ letters. Submit to pop them; the tiles above fall and new letters drop in at the top.

Scoring: each letter has a Scrabble-style value (E/A = 1, Q/Z = 10). Length bonus: 3 = 10 · 4 = 20 · 5 = 40 · 6 = 80 · 7 = 160 · 8+ = 320+. Valid words in a row build a combo multiplier.

Hazards (classic): red burning tiles sink a row every turn. Use them in a word to put them out; if one reaches the bottom, the spire collapses.

Rewards: 5-letter words gild a tile (2x), 6 jewel one (3x), 7 make a sapphire (4x), 8+ a ruby (5x).

Mouse     click tiles in order; click again to back up
          double-click the last tile, or Submit
Keyboard  arrows move · Space add · Enter submit
          Backspace remove last · Esc / P pause
