HOW TO PLAY

Chain adjacent letter tiles (orthogonal or diagonal) into valid English words of 3+ letters. Submit to pop them — the column above each popped tile falls, and new letters spawn at the top.

Scoring: each letter has a Scrabble-ish value (E/A=1, Q/Z=10). Length bonus: 3=10 · 4=20 · 5=40 · 6=80 · 7=160 · 8+=320+. Consecutive valid submissions build a combo multiplier.

Hazards (classic): red burning tiles drop one row every turn. Include them in a word to extinguish them — if one reaches the bottom, the spire collapses.

Rewards: 5+ letter words spawn gilded (2x) or jeweled (3x) tiles. 7+ letter words spawn sapphire (4x); 8+ letter words spawn ruby (5x).

Controls:
Mouse: click tiles in sequence; click again to backtrack; double-click last tile or the Submit button to submit.
Keyboard: arrows move cursor, Space adds the current tile, Enter submits, Backspace removes last. Esc / P to pause.
