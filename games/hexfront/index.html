How to Play

You command the red army. Destroy every blue unit.

Click a red unit to select. Blue cells show move range; red-lit enemies can be attacked. After moving, pick a target or click elsewhere to hold position.

E ends your turn (blue AI acts). S/L save and load. Forests and hills cost more movement; high ground deals +25% damage.

Esc / P pause.
