    How to Play
    
            
    
                
    Hearthfolk is an observatory sim. Five villagers live by their needs —
                work, eat, sleep, talk, tend the fire — through day and night.
    
                
    Click a villager to open their mind panel (goals, thoughts, memories).
                Arrows / WASD pan, wheel zooms.
    
                
    Space pauses the sim. 1 / 4 set speed.
                Save and load from the status bar. Esc / P open the pause menu.
