How to Play

A / D or ← / → to run.

Space (or W / ↑) to jump. Hold for a higher jump.

J / K / F or click to fire the beam (you start armed).

Land on a stomper to squash it. Touch one from the side and you lose a life.

Falling off the bottom is also bad. Reach the flag at the right edge to win.
