HOW TO PLAY

The foreman watches the farm and assigns Mara, Tom and Lily to feed the animals, water and harvest the crops, and gather produce. The farm sustains itself — you can pitch in any time.

You are the farmer in the straw hat. Walk up to anything that needs doing and interact:

WASD / Arrows     Move
E / Space         Interact with what you're next to
                  (fill troughs, harvest, water, plant,
                   collect produce, draw water, load feed)
Esc / P           Pause
