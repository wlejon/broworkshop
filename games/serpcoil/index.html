How to Play

A serpent of coloured runes marches toward the maw. Fire runes from the eye; three or more of a colour break from the chain, and the gap pulls the front back, maybe into another match.

Aim     Mouse / Left, Right
Fire    Left click / Space
Swap    Right click / Shift
Pause   Esc / P

Powerups ride your next rune every few dozen breaks:
Backtrack · Blaster · Colorshift · Slow-mo
