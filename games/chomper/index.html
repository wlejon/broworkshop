HOW TO PLAY

Guide Chomper through the maze and eat all the pellets.

Controls

Arrows / WASD   Move
Esc / P         Pause
Enter           Confirm

Pellets are worth 10 points.
Power pellets (large dots) are worth 50 and frighten ghosts.

Eat frightened ghosts for 200 → 400 → 800 → 1600 points.

You start with 3 lives. Touching a normal ghost costs a life.
