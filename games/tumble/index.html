How to Play

Goal: Get any marble from the gold spout into the green cup.

Build: Pick a piece on the left, click the board to place it under / between spout and cup. Right-click removes. R rotates.

Run: Space drops marbles. Timer stops when the first marble enters the cup. Space again rebuilds.

Pieces: Block (floor) · Ramp (slope) · Wall · Bumper (bounce) · Spinner · Booster (arrow = shove) · Chute (funnel).

Camera: right-drag orbit · middle pan · wheel zoom.

Clear levels to unlock the next. Beat gold / silver / bronze pars for medals.
