How to Play

Gold spout drops marbles. Green cup is the goal. Spend your piece budget so a marble lands in the cup.

Camera: right-drag orbit · middle-drag pan · wheel zoom.

Build: 1–7 piece · click place · right-click remove · R rotate · Q/E layer.

Run: Space drops marbles (again to rebuild). Timer stops on first cup hit.

Pieces: Block · Ramp · Wall · Bumper · Spinner · Booster · Chute — later levels introduce each tool.

Aim: Booster arrow = shove direction. Ramp slope runs downhill from the high end — use R.

Medals: gold / silver / bronze pars. Clear a level to unlock the next. Eight levels total.
