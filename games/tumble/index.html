How to Play

Goal: marble from gold spout → green cup.

Place: pick a piece on the left, click a grid cell on the floor. Green highlight = can place. Right-click removes.

Rotate: R turns ramps and boosters. Booster arrow = shove direction.

Run: Space drops marbles. Space again rebuilds.

Camera: right-drag look · wheel zoom · middle pan.
