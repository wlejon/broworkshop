How to Play

Build towers to stop creeps reaching the golden keep. There is no fixed path — creeps route around your maze.

1 / 2 / 3 arm Arrow, Cannon, or Frost. Click grass to place. You cannot fully wall off the path. Hilltops give +1 range.

Click a tower to upgrade (U) or sell (X). Space starts the next wave. Survive all 10 waves.

Esc / P pause. Right-click cancels placement.
