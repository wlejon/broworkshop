HOW TO PLAY

Gravity is pulling you down. Burn fuel to slow your descent and steer toward a flat landing pad.

Land gently, upright, and on a flat pad to score. Narrow pads are worth more.

Controls

Up / Space    Thrust
Left / Right  Rotate
R-Click       Mouse thrust + aim
Esc / P       Pause

Safe landing requires
Low vertical speed, low horizontal speed,
nearly upright, and on a flat pad.
