HOW TO PLAY

Swap two adjacent gems to line up 3 or more of the same kind.
Matched gems clear; gems above fall to fill the gaps.

Scoring: 3=50 · 4=100 · 5+=150
Each cascade multiplies the next: x1, x2, x3...

Special gems:
Match 4 in a line → Flame Gem (3x3 burst)
Match 5 in L/T shape → Starlight Gem (row + column)
Match 5 in a line → Hypergem (clears one color)

Controls:
Click a gem, then click an adjacent gem to swap.
Or use arrow keys + Space/Enter to pick up and swap.
Esc / P to pause.
