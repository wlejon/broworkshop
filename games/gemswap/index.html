How to Play

Swap two neighbouring gems to line up 3 or more of a kind. Matched gems shatter and the gems above fall into the gaps.

Scoring: 3 = 50 · 4 = 100 · 5+ = 150. Each cascade step multiplies: x1, x2, x3…

Special gems
4 in a line → Flame Gem (3x3 burst)
L or T shape → Starlight Gem (row + column)
5 in a line → Hypergem (swap it to clear one color)

Frozen gems can't be swapped and hold the gems above them. A match next to or through them cracks the ice.

Mouse     click a gem, then a neighbour
          (or drag onto the neighbour)
Keyboard  arrows move · Space / Enter pick
          arrows swap the picked gem
Esc / P   pause
