HOW TO PLAY

Stacks of colored blocks rise from the bottom. Your carrier floats along the top and can pick up the topmost block of any column.

Press Down to pick up the top block, move, then press Down again to drop it. Whenever a column's top 3+ blocks share a color (including across neighboring columns), they pop.

Scoring: 3-pop=150 · 4=300 · 5+=600.
Cascades multiply the next pop: x1, x2, x3...

Specials:
Star pops every block of its landing color.
Bomb explodes a 3x3 area.
Rainbow matches as any color.

Controls:
←/→ or A/D move carrier. ↓/S pick/drop. ↑/W shuffle held stack.
Space: emergency brake (slows rise for 3s, once per 10s).
Mouse: click column to move there & pick/drop. Wheel to shuffle.
Esc / P: pause.
