Screen-space reflections

Mirror strip at the front of the courtyard is the test surface. Tilt down until the spheres leave the frame — their reflection dies with them. That is the screen-space limit.

Reflection probe

The chrome sphere is near-black with the probe off: this courtyard has no IBL environment, so a metal has nothing to reflect until a probe gives it one.
SSR vs probe. SSR is accurate but screen-bound — only what is on screen reflects, and hits fade at the borders. The probe is stable and works off-screen, but it is a static box-projected cubemap. SSR wins where its ray hits; the probe is the miss fallback. Toggle each alone to see where one gives up and the other takes over.

Projected decals

Left-click the scene to stamp one. It raycasts to the surface and orients the projection to the hit normal — walls take them sideways, floors top-down.
