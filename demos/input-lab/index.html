Action bindings bro.settings.defineAction / rebindAction

Click any binding chip to rebind it, then press a key, a mouse button, a gamepad button, or push a stick past 60%. Esc cancels. All three binding kinds — "a", "mouse:left", "gamepad:righttrigger", "gamepad:leftx+" — live in the same list.

Analog proof the same actions, driving something

Thrust and turn are read with getActionStrength(), not isActionPressed(). On a keyboard they are 0 or 1 and the ship snaps; on a stick or trigger they are continuous and it eases. Same action, same code path.
