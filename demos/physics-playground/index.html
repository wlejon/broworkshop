Simulation

Physics steps at a fixed rate; rendering does not. Drag the step rate down to 15–20 Hz and watch a falling object: with interpolation off it teleports once per step. Turn it on and the same simulation glides, because the render side blends the previous and current step poses by the frame's accumulator fraction. The simulation is identical either way — only what you SEE changes.

Pose drive

not driving

driveToPose puts position motors on every swing-twist joint and lets gravity keep working — the ragdoll develops muscle tone but still falls, sags and gets pushed around. Set it once; the motors persist until go limp. Kinematic drive instead sets part velocities so each part reaches its target transform every step: it stands the figure up and shoves whatever is in the way. Drop a ragdoll, punch it over, then drive it with the checkbox off and on.

Soft bodies

Pinned vertices carry invMass 0 — they do not move at all while the sheet between them sags. Drop boxes into the hammock, then switch to one edge and watch the same sheet drape.

Pressurized ball

The gas coefficient of a closed mesh. At 300 the ball is a wet bag that slaps the lane and stays there; at 6000 it is a drum and rebounds most of the way back. There is no runtime setter, so moving the slider rebuilds and re-drops the ball — which is what you want for an A/B anyway.

Combine modes

Two touching bodies each carry their own friction and restitution, but a contact needs ONE of each. The combine mode decides. Drop a high-restitution ball on the rubber lane (0.92) and on the ice lane (0.05), then switch between min and max: under max the ball bounces on both, under min it dies on the ice. default is Jolt's own rule — sqrt(f1·f2) for friction, max(r1,r2) for restitution.

Collision layers

Row/column = layer. A cleared box means that pair is rejected in the broadphase — the two never touch. Changes apply to bodies that already exist, so untick projectile / scenery and watch pink objects fall straight through the ramps and walls.

Machines (SixDOF)

A SixDOF constraint is six independent switches — three translations, three rotations — each locked, limited to a range, or free, and each able to carry a motor. That is not a joint, that is a machine tool.

Motors

Slew is a velocity motor: set a rate and the crane turns forever. Winch and piston are position motors: set a place and the machine goes there and holds it — load the platform and it still holds it.

Bars at the pivot, X red / Y green / Z blue: a short dark stub is locked, a medium bar is limited, a long glowing bar is free. Rotation axes wear a collar of pips. Flip tY on the piston to free and the platform falls off its own lift.

—

Two 0.5 m spheres on a rope capped at 0.4 m — shorter than the sum of their radii, so the flag decides which wins. Off (the default) the pair is excluded from collision for the constraint's lifetime and the spheres merge; on, contact survives the joint and pushes them apart past the rope's own limit.

Mechanism bench

gear, rackAndPinion and pulley all shipped in bro's binding layer and nothing had ever called them. Note the shape of the first two: they couple two existing constraint handles rather than building their own joints.

The pulley needs no motor at all: 120 kg on one end, 25 kg on the other, one rope of fixed total length over two pivots.

Breakable joints

broken / total—

—

Every joint in the bridge carries a breakingImpulse in N·s. When the impulse the solver needed to hold that joint closed exceeds it in one step, Jolt disables the constraint and getBrokenConstraints() reports its handle — a whole destruction system for one number per joint. The hangers go first, their load transfers to their neighbours, and the deck unzips.

Contacts & impacts

events/frame0
impact force 0 N·s

An added event carries the solver's own manifold: up to four world-space points, the contact normal, penetration depth, and an impulse estimate in kg·m/s. A negative penetration is a speculative contact — Jolt predicted a touch it has not solved yet — and the list labels those spec. The impulse is the interesting one: it scales with mass and closing speed, so it is the natural input for impact sounds, damage and particles. Here it drives all three effects above. Turn the drawing off and the sparks still fire; turn the effects off and the data is still drawn. Same stream, read two ways.
