Animation

The clip drives every part kinematically: the parts are real bodies moving at the clip's velocities, so a runner that gets hit keeps its momentum.

Actions

Left-drag a limb to grab it: the figure goes limp from that limb. Right-drag orbits, middle-drag pans, the wheel zooms.
