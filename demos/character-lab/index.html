On: an overlapShape standing where you stand returns you. Off: it finds nothing, and every sensing ray stops hitting SELF. The ball is not affected by this checkbox — CharacterVirtual resolves its own contacts either way. Push strength decides it: at 0 N the same throw sails clean through you.
